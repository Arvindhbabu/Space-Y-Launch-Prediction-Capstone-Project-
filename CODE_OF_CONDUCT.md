# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in SpaceY Intelligence should be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Give and receive technical feedback professionally.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and focus on ideas rather than people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's personal information.
- Acknowledge source material and other contributors' work.
- Help maintain a learning environment for students, researchers, and practitioners.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and persistently disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on keeping the project safe and constructive.

To report a concern, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other project spaces associated with SpaceY Intelligence.
